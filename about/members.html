THE MEMBERS

Three friends who've been making music together since childhood.

Dylan Minnette

VOCALS · RHYTHM GUITAR · KEYBOARDS

Dylan is the lead vocalist and rhythm guitarist of Wallows. Beyond music, he is also known as an actor, having appeared in numerous TV shows and films. Born in Evansville, Indiana, he moved to LA where he met his future bandmates.

Braeden Lemasters

VOCALS · LEAD GUITAR · BASS

Braeden handles lead guitar duties and shares vocal responsibilities with Dylan. Also an actor by trade, he brings a creative and cinematic perspective to Wallows' songwriting and visual aesthetic.

Cole Preston

DRUMS · GUITAR · KEYBOARDS · BACKING VOCALS

Cole is the powerhouse behind the kit, providing the rhythmic backbone to Wallows' sound. A multi-instrumentalist, he also contributes guitar and keyboard parts, making him one of the most versatile members of the group.
