ABOUT WALLOWS

The story behind the indie rock sensation.

Origins

Wallows is an American alternative rock band from Los Angeles, California. Formed by childhood friends, the trio has been playing music together since they were 11 years old. What started in a small bedroom jam session has evolved into a global phenomenon.

Known for their energetic live performances, nostalgic soundscapes, and deeply relatable lyrics, they continue to push the boundaries of modern indie music.
