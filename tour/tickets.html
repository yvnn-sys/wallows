TICKETING FAQ

Got questions about VIP packages, resale, or age restrictions? Read below.

When will VIP details (soundcheck time, entry) be sent?

All VIP package details, schedule, and entry instructions will be emailed to the ticket buyer 3-5 days before the event. Please check your spam folder if you do not receive it.

Can I purchase tickets on behalf of a friend?

Yes, but for VIP packages, the photo ID of the original purchaser may be required at check-in. If you bought tickets as a gift, please make sure they bring a copy of your ID and order confirmation.

Are tickets refundable?

All ticket sales are final and non-refundable unless the show is cancelled or rescheduled. In case of cancellation, refunds will be issued automatically by the ticketing service provider.
